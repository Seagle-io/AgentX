Why Seagle?

At Seagle, we believe artificial intelligence should be accessible to all, without technical or financial barriers.

Our MissionOur Mission

We are convinced that artificial intelligence should not be a luxury reserved for large corporations. Our mission is to democratise access to AI by creating simple, intuitive and affordable solutions, capable of competing with the tools of large organisations, without their constraints or cost.

01

Seagle advocates for sovereign AI: models hosted in Europe, data under French control, architectures free from extraterritorial hyperscalers. Europe has the talent and values to build a credible alternative to American and Chinese giants. We contribute to this, project by project.

02

AI consumes resources. Data centres, mass inference, energy-intensive training runs: the carbon cost of digital technology is real and growing. At Seagle, we design frugal systems: optimised models, local inference where possible, architectures sized to actual needs. AI must be a lever for ecological transition, not an additional obstacle on the path to a sustainable world.

“
Our deepest conviction: AI must enrich human intellect, not replace it. Delegating your thinking to a machine is to diminish yourself. Using the machine to think further, faster, more clearly : that is emancipation. Every tool we build is designed to amplify your capabilities, sharpen your judgement, and keep you in control. The human remains the centre of gravity of everything we do.

That is why we are committed to democratising artificial intelligence through open-source products and AI solutions, efficient, accessible, and built to last.
